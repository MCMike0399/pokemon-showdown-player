\section{Limitations and research directions}
\label{sec:limits}

The project now has a controlled live gain for a team change, in addition to
its early offline search result. It does not have a proven process for
continually improving turn decisions, competitive-level play, or an
end-to-end optimal policy. The unsuccessful campaigns constrain the next
questions more usefully than another list of promising offline metrics.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Transfer from a surrogate remains the main research gap.}
 Better outcome log loss, reply accuracy, probe retention and sampled-game
 security each failed to predict stronger play in specific campaigns. C12's
 small duality gaps certify its finite matrices, not correct leaves, beliefs,
 missing replies or information-consistent continuations. Future value work
 should distinguish prediction from counterfactual action ranking and preserve
 information constraints across hidden worlds \citep{brown2018depth}.
 \item \textbf{C13 identifies a team--pilot interaction.}
 Its +61.9-Elo estimate concerns two exact teams under C5 search and C4
 preview on this ladder. It is not an estimate of human tournament team
 quality, an improvement in learned turn weights, or a decomposition into
 individual species effects. Optional stopping and selecting challengers can
 inflate the winning estimate. The subsequent $1344\pm33$ cohort is useful
 follow-up evidence but not another randomized comparison.
 \item \textbf{C14 remains an experiment.}
 The bot rarely using Venusaur is a diagnostic, and human usage is an
 observational lower bound on being brought. Making the bot resemble humans
 does not guarantee more wins: C9 already changed Protect frequencies without
 demonstrating an advantage. Retain the fixed stopping rule instead of
 promoting from appealing plans or a short rating streak.
 \item \textbf{Sequential uncertainty needs stronger calibration.}
 The normal-approximation live test has nominal .05/.10 parameters but is
 not an exact sequential likelihood-ratio test. Repeated opponents, rating
 feedback, factorial interactions, variance estimation and several successive
 campaigns complicate error rates. Ordinary performance intervals do not
 account for repeated looks. A future protocol could preregister block-aware
 inference or anytime-valid confidence sequences and a family-wise error
 budget; those methods are not implemented or retrospectively credited here.
 \item \textbf{Missingness and treatment delivery need continued auditing.}
 Unfinished rooms are excluded until a terminal arrives, while forfeit wins
 count. Outages could still differ by arm. Compare assignment-to-terminal
 rates, baseline fallbacks and actual team/preview delivery in addition to
 completed outcomes. A source fix, browser repair or model change during a
 segment should not silently become a strategic gain.
 \item \textbf{Beliefs and state remain approximate.}
 Usage marginals omit correlations among moves, items, spreads and teammates.
 Speed constraints and public facts help but do not make a calibrated
 posterior. Mega forcing, omitted opponent PP, incomplete history restoration,
 unseeded duration draws and determinized continuation decisions remain
 limitations. Correlated-set beliefs and information-consistent continuation
 policies are research candidates requiring fresh tests, not established fixes.
 \item \textbf{Compute is a means, not a result.}
 The M5 accelerates fitting, but CPU simulator evaluation dominates campaign
 time. C6 did not validate spending more compute on the same estimator, and
 C10's screening efficiency did not make its combined endgame candidate win.
 Further hardware optimization should preserve the evaluated policy and
 report simulation cost, latency, legality and strength separately.
\end{enumerate}

The software's strongest achievement is an auditable loop from a recorded
failure to a fixed candidate, frozen inputs, persistent evaluation and an
explicit deployment decision. Its scientific achievement at this cutoff is
narrower and concrete: a team change beat the prior team in a controlled live
comparison after many plausible search changes did not. The remaining task is
to turn more accurate models and better search objectives into similarly
measured improvements, without rewriting negative results as successes.
